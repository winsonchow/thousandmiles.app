Three reads

Every mile tells you something.

Let's make sense of it. Three reads, each against your own usual, never a population norm.

  1. 01 Readiness

    Good day for a big effort

    Sleep, recovery and how hard the week has been, in one morning headline. A watch makes it richer; a rating of your morning is enough to start.

  2. 02 Journal

    Keep the feel, not just the number

    Workouts, meals, mood and weigh-ins with a photo and a few words. Health workouts arrive on their own; you add how they went.

  3. 03 Your Goals

    One change a week

    Set a goal, then each Monday see last week against the path and the single thing worth changing. No streaks, no pressure.

We can't read your journal. That's the design.

A few plain promises, each one in the privacy policy.

  • No ads.The app shows none, free or Plus.
  • No analytics, and nothing sold.Your health data never goes to advertisers or data brokers.
  • Your journal is sealed on your phone.Encrypted with a key in your iCloud Keychain. Our server keeps a copy it can't open.
  • Apple Health data never leaves your phone.It's read on your iPhone and never uploaded.
  • AI asks first, and sees only what you give it.What you type or pick, or the meals you logged for a weekly read. Never your Health data. Anthropic doesn't train on it, and you can switch AI off any time.
  • No password to leak.Sign in with a code by email, or with Apple.
  • Export or delete everything, any time.Both from the app. Deleting your account removes your server data immediately.
  • Stored in Singapore, under Singapore's PDPA.Questions: privacy@thousandmiles.app
