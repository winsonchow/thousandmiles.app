How the morning read works

What goes in

Three things, with sleep counting most, then recovery, then freshness.

  • Sleep. The main sleep that ended this morning, read as one night: how long you slept against your goal, your last two weeks of nights, how settled the night was and how regular your bedtimes are.
  • Recovery. Heart-rate variability, resting heart rate and wrist temperature, taken while you slept, when your watch records them.
  • Freshness. Your recent training against what you're used to. The same measure as Training load on the Training page.

Against your own usual

Each night is compared with the 21 nights before it, never with an average for your age. Recovery needs seven nights to learn from before it counts.

How you feel

Each morning you can pick one of four statements, from I feel really good to I'm running on empty. With your watch's readings, your answer moves the read a little, and I feel fine leaves it as it is. No watch, or a reading missing? Your answer stands in for it.

It keeps up with your day

A session you finish today counts straight away, from your rating, your heart rate or the kind of activity. A walk counts as light.

Private by design

  • Your journal is sealed on your phone.What you write is encrypted before it is backed up, with a key only your devices hold.
  • AI asks first.It sees only what you give it: a meal's words or photos, the choices for a workout you describe, or the meals you logged. Never your Health data or training history.
  • No ads, no analytics.And we never sell your data.
  • No password to leak.Sign in with a code by email, or with Apple.
  • Export or delete everything, any time.Both from the app.
