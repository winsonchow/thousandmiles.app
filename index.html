A little further than yesterday

We can't read your journal. That's the design.

Entries, photos and notes are sealed on your phone with a key kept in your iCloud Keychain. Our server holds them for your other devices and cannot open them. Health data never leaves your phone, and the AI features never see it or your training history: they ask first and get only what you type. No analytics. Stored in Singapore. Export or delete everything from Settings.
