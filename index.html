Every morning

Know where you're starting from.

Last night's sleep, how you've recovered and how hard your week has been, read each morning into one plain sentence about today. A watch makes it richer. If you don't wear one, tell it how you slept.

"Take a lighter day."

Some mornings say go, some say ease off. Either way it's one sentence, not a dashboard, and each part of your morning is read in words rather than figures.

Against your own usual. Every read compares you with your own recent nights, never with someone else.

On your Home Screen. A widget carries this morning's read, so it's there before you open the app.

In the moment

Every session, as it happens.

Lift set by set with last time beside you. Runs, rides and swims arrive from your Watch with the route, pace and heart-rate zones. Bests show up the moment you make them.

  • Strength, set by set. Last time's sets beside today's, a weight to try, and the rest timer on your Lock Screen while you catch your breath.

  • Runs and rides from your Watch. Workouts from Apple Watch, or any app that writes to Apple Health, arrive on their own with the route, pace and heart-rate zones. You add how it felt.

  • Lunch from a photo. Point the camera at your plate. Calories, protein, carbs and fat are worked out for you, and marked as an estimate. Plus

  • Your week at the table. Each week, a short read of what you ate: what ran high, what fell short and what went well. Plus

The morning read

One sentence. A lot behind it.

What goes in

Three things, with sleep counting most, then recovery, then freshness.

  • SleepThe main sleep that ended this morning, read as one night.
  • RecoveryHeart-rate variability, resting heart rate and wrist temperature, taken while you slept, when your watch records them.
  • FreshnessYour recent training against what you're used to. The same measure as the Training load card in Insights.

Against your own usual

Each night is compared with the 21 nights before it, never with an average for your age. Recovery stays neutral until it has five nights to learn from.

No watch? Still a full read

Answer two quick questions about how you slept and how you feel, and your own rating stands in for the missing parts.

It keeps up with your day

A session you finish today counts straight away, from your rating, your Watch's effort or your heart rate. A walk counts as light.

Your Goals

Eight roads. Pick yours.

One goal free, up to three with Plus. Each Monday, last week is read against it.

  • Get fasterA distance in a time
  • Go furtherA longer run, ride or swim
  • Get strongerA lift, or across your lifts
  • Build muscleLean mass, every muscle trained enough
  • Lose weightTo a weight, at a steady rate
  • Keep my weightStay in a range
  • Be consistentSessions a week
  • Sleep betterHours, and a steady bedtime

What Monday looks like

Last complete week against the path to your goal, whether you're on course, the one change worth making, and what fed the read. If something's missing, it says so instead of guessing.
