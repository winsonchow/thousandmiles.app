Getting started

How do I sign in?

Enter your email and we send a one-time code, so there's no password. You can also continue with Apple. An account is what lets your journal survive a reinstall and reach your other devices.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Any watch or tracker that writes to Apple Health works: Garmin, Oura, Whoop, Fitbit, Polar, Coros and others. With no tracker, the readiness card asks two quick questions each morning instead.

How do I change what Thousand Miles can read from Apple Health?

Open the Settings app → Health → Data Access & Devices → Thousand Miles, and switch each type on or off. The app can't show Apple's permission sheet a second time, so this is the way back if you declined it.

Thousand Miles Plus

How do I cancel or change my plan?

In the Settings app, tap your name → Subscriptions → Thousand Miles Plus. You can also open it from Profile → Thousand Miles Plus → Manage subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next renewal; Plus stays active until then.

How do I get a refund?

Apple processes every purchase and handles all refunds: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds ourselves.

I paid, but Plus isn't showing.

Open Profile → Thousand Miles Plus → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account you paid with. If you have an offer code, use Redeem a code on the same page.

A new phone

Will my journal come with me?

Yes. Sign in with the same account. Your journal is encrypted with a key kept in your iCloud Keychain, so a phone signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud Keychain on opens it straight away. Apple Health data moves through Apple's own iCloud Health sync.

My new phone says some of my journal is locked.

That phone doesn't have your key yet. Turn on iCloud Keychain (Settings → your name → iCloud → Passwords and Keychain) and give it a minute, or choose Enter your recovery code on the sheet the app shows. Nothing is deleted while it's locked. If every device, your iCloud Keychain and your recovery code are all lost, we can't unlock it for you, because we never had the key.

Your data

How do I export everything?

Settings → Privacy → Export My Data. You get a zip of every entry, your profile, settings and goals as readable JSON, plus your photos. Apple Health data isn't in it; the Health app has its own export.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete Account. This removes your data from our servers and clears the app on that phone. Nothing in Apple Health is touched. Export first if you want a copy.

What do you do with my data?

As little as possible. Apple Health data never leaves your phone, and what you write is encrypted before it reaches our servers. The Privacy Policy lists every detail.

Please note

Thousand Miles is a journal, not a medical device. Readiness scores, recaps and nutrition estimates are informational and don't replace a qualified clinician. See the Terms of Service.
